Change Log

A record of updates to the MCU Viewing Order - new additions, order adjustments, and site improvements.

June 30, 2026 Added a way to reset your progress
Update
  • A Reset button now sits next to the progress bar, letting you mark everything as unwatched in one click. You'll be asked to confirm first, since it can't be undone unless you've exported a progress code.
June 26, 2026 Added Blade: The Series & reordered some Fox/Sony films
New Fix
  • Blade: The Series has been added to the viewing order.
  • Slightly adjusted the order of Spider-Man 3, Fantastic Four (2005), and Elektra on the Timeline and Sacred Timeline views.
June 21, 2026 Added new shows & fixed airdates
New Fix
  • Iron Man: The Animated Series, Fantastic Four: The Animated Series, and The Incredible Hulk (1996) have been added to the viewing order.
  • Corrected several episode airdates for X-Men: The Animated Series and Spider-Man: The Animated Series that didn't match their actual broadcast dates.
  • Spider-Man (1994) has been renamed to Spider-Man: The Animated Series for consistency.
June 5, 2026 Reclassified Team Thor shorts
Fix
  • Team Thor, Team Thor: Part 2, and Team Darryl have been reclassified as One Shots to match Disney+'s current designation.
June 1, 2026 Added Sacred Timeline order
Update
  • The Sacred Timeline sort order has been added as a viewing option.
May 30, 2026 Added Spider-Man (1994)
New
  • Spider-Man (1994) has been added to the viewing order.
May 27, 2026 Added Spider-Noir
New
  • Spider-Noir has been added to the viewing order.
