How do I get Hoardware?

Download Hoardware for free from the App Store on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad and give your Apple collection a home.

Which devices can I add to my collection?

Hoardware uses AppleDB’s catalog, covering Mac, iPhone, iPad, iPod, Apple Watch, Apple TV, HomePod, AirPods, and more. The hardware you collect doesn’t need to run Hoardware itself.

Where does my collection live?

Your library syncs through your private iCloud database. Optional shared links and release alerts use Hoardware services with limited data, as explained in the privacy policy. You can also export your library and create a backup.

What do I need to run it?

Hoardware supports macOS Sonoma 14 or later, and iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Check the App Store listing for the latest compatibility details.

How can I share feedback?

To share feedback or report a bug, email Aaron. Bug reports and ideas from fellow collectors help shape what comes next.
