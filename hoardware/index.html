How do I try Hoardware?

Install Apple’s TestFlight app on your Mac, iPhone, or iPad, then open the beta invitation and follow the installation steps. Hoardware is currently in beta, so features may change as development continues.

Which devices can I add to my collection?

Hoardware uses AppleDB’s catalog, covering Mac, iPhone, iPad, iPod, Apple Watch, Apple TV, HomePod, AirPods, and more. The hardware you collect doesn’t need to run Hoardware itself.

Where does my collection live?

Your library syncs through your private iCloud database. Hoardware has no server of its own. You can also export your library and create a backup.

What do I need to run it?

Hoardware supports macOS Sonoma 14 or later, and iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. Check the TestFlight invitation for the requirements and availability of the current beta build.

How can I share beta feedback?

Send feedback from Hoardware’s page in TestFlight, or email Aaron. Bug reports and ideas from fellow collectors help shape what comes next.
